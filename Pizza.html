Pizza


Origin of Pizza


Pizza is a beloved Italian dish that has become a global favorite. Originating from Naples, Italy,it traditionally features a thin, hand-tossed dough topped

with rich tomato sauce, creamy mozzarella cheese, and a variety of toppings--from pepperoni and mushrooms to fresh basil and olives. Baked to perfection in

a hot oven, pizza combines crispy edges, gooey cheese, and bold flavors in every bite.

Today, pizza is enjoyed in many styles--Neapolitan, New York, Chicago deep-dish--making it a versatile and delicious meal for any occasion.


Ingredients

  • Pizza dough (flour, yeast, water, salt, olive oil)
  • Tomato sauce (or pizza sauce)
  • Mozzarella cheese (shredded or fresh)
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic (minced or powdered)
  • Dried oregano or Italian seasoning
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Toppings (e.g., pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, olives, sausage, etc.)
  • Cornmeal or flour (for dusting the baking surface)

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough: Mix flour, yeast, water, salt and olive oil. Knead and let it rise for 1-2 hours.
  2. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 220 degrees celsius or (425 degrees Fahrenheit) and let it heat up.
  3. Roll out the dough: On a floured surface, roll the dough into your desired shape and thickness.
  4. Add sauce: Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  5. Add chesse and toppings: Sprinkle mozzarella and add your favorite toppings (e.g., pepperoni, mushrooms, etc.).
  6. Bake: Transfer to a baking tray or pizza stone and bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
  7. Serve: Let it cool slightly, slice, and enjoy your homemade pizza!
